## 💡 Executive Summary of Solution: ECO-CONNECT

Telkomsel faces a severe **Monetization Squeeze**: annual data traffic surges by **+25%**, while blended ARPU stagnates at **Rp49,000** and market share slipped to **44%**. Concurrently, ±350 physical GraPARI branches handle **15 million walk-in visits/year**, incurring massive operating expenses (**~Rp375 Billion/year**) and generating **~37,500 tons of $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$** ($2.5\text{ kg CO}_2\text{e}$ per visit, driven by customer travel).

**ECO-CONNECT** solves this through three synergistic pillars:

### Key Quantitative Impact Metrics (3-Year Horizon):
- 🌿 **Avoided Carbon Emissions:** **37,544 Tons of $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$** avoided (Scope 3 GHG Protocol).
- ⏱️ **Societal Productivity:** **6.25 Million hours** of customer travel and queuing eradicated.
- 💰 **GraPARI Cost-to-Serve Savings:** **Rp 172.5 Billion / year** in operational savings.
- 📈 **New Monetized Revenue:** **Rp 306.0 Billion / year** from AI VAS & Eco-Pass partnerships.
- 💵 **Net Present Value (NPV @10% WACC):** **Rp 672.4 Billion**.
- 🚀 **Internal Rate of Return (IRR):** **142%** | **Payback Period:** **7.8 Months**.
- 📱 **Active Digital ARPU Lift:** **+Rp 3,000 / active user**.
