# PREFACE

## What is IDSC

International Development Student Conference (IDSC) is an annual competition held by the Faculty of Economics and Business, Universitas Airlangga. IDSC thrives to develop scientific papers and problem solving of Business Cases to educate the masses about the most recent phenomenon.

The expected output of this event is to provide opportunities for national and international undergraduate students to challenge themselves in scientific papers and problem solving of Business Cases. The purpose of this event is to raise the creativity, skills, and quality of students in handling global economic problems that are currently happening based on SDGs number 4 (Quality Education), SDGs number 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth), SDGs number 9 (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure), and SDGs number 10 (Reduced Inequalities).

## What is IBCC

The International Business Case Competition (IBCC) is international level competition for active students from both national and international universities who have an interest in compiling papers, creating business solutions and conferencing.

# GRAND THEME

“Future-Proofing The Economy: Digital Resilience and Inclusive Growth In Emerging Markets”

Amid rapid digital transformation and shifting global economic dynamics, emerging markets face increasing challenges in maintaining sustainable and inclusive economic growth. While technology and innovation continue to accelerate economic opportunities, issues such as inequality, economic vulnerability, and uneven digital access remain significant concerns. As future changemakers, students play an important role in contributing innovative ideas and strategic solutions toward building resilient and inclusive economies.

Through the theme “Future-Proofing The Economy: Digital Resilience and Inclusive Growth in Emerging Markets,” the 15th International Development Student Conference (IDSC) serves as an international platform for students to explore global economic issues through the International Business Case Competition (IBCC), Conference & Call for Paper (CCFP), and National Seminar. By encouraging critical thinking, collaboration, and innovation, IDSC 2026 aims to empower youth to actively contribute toward sustainable development while supporting the implementation of SDGs 4, 8, 9, and 10.

<!-- PDF page 5 -->

# SUB THEME

“Leveraging Inclusive Technology to Drive Sustainable Economic Transformation”

This subtheme emphasizes the role of inclusive technology as a key driver of sustainable economic transformation in an increasingly digital world. Participants are encouraged to develop innovative solutions that leverage technology to expand economic opportunities, enhance productivity, and ensure that the benefits of development are accessible to diverse communities. By adopting a sustainability-oriented approach, the proposed solutions are expected to address current economic challenges while fostering resilient, inclusive, and long-term economic growth.

# EVENT SERIES

## Preliminary Stage

Each team is required to submit an executive summary, following the guidelines, that summarizes their solution to the given business case, which will be provided as a Simulated Business Case Challenge released on (TBA).

## Semifinal Stage

Teams advancing from the preliminary round will proceed to the next stage, which requires the development and submission of a comprehensive full paper proposal. The proposal must articulate a well-structured business solution to the case provided by the Company Collaborator, supported by thorough analysis, strategic recommendations, and relevant evidence.

# EVENT SERIES

## Final Stage

Teams that successfully passed the Semi Final (full proposal selection) stage will be informed via team leader's email address. A total of (approximately) 6 teams with the best Full Proposal will qualify for the final stage. The official announcement of finalists will be on November 13th 2026. Finalists will be granted the opportunity to:

### Final Pitch

Each finalist will present their business case proposal before the panel of judges, showcasing their in-depth analysis and proposed solutions.

### Conference

During the Conference session, the committee will provide a live case from a company collaborator for each team to solve. All teams will then present their proposed solutions in an open conference forum alongside other participants. Each finalist will also serve as a panelist during the Conference session.

## Awarding Night

This is the closing of the entire IBCC and CCFP 2026. Awarding Night was held to express appreciation for the dedication and achievements of the participants. The competition winners will be officially announced and presented with awards according to the contested categories.

# SEMIFINAL STAGE

- Executive Summary - Contains a maximum of 2 pages.
- Introduction - The introduction includes company background, research objectives about company implications, and a discussion section (if any).
- Literature Review - Literature review contains the theoretical basis and relevant company concepts from clear sources, including previous opinions, innovations and problem solving that have been done by the company.
- Research Method - The research method contains the procedures used in writing the scientific paper, including how to collect and process data and information. This section must also highlight the Framework Analysis as the main approach to examine the company's condition and problem context.
- Strategy & Solution - The strategy & solutions contains an analysis of problems based on data and information that has been reviewed in the literature review to produce problem solving from the business case.
- Implementation Roadmap & KPI
- Financial Projection
- Closing - Consists of conclusions and suggestions.
- Reference
- Attachment
